# RFC-011: Retry policy for checkout-api to payments-svc

**Status:** accepted · **Author:** Marcus Oyelaran · **Reviewers:** Priya Raman, Tomás Ortega

## Decision
checkout-api retries calls to payments-svc at most twice, with exponential backoff starting at 400 ms and full jitter, behind a circuit breaker (opossum, 50% error threshold over 10 s). This is the `standard` value of the `checkout.retry_policy` flag.

## Why
Retries multiply load exactly when the downstream is struggling. With six attempts and a 50 ms backoff, a payments-svc slowdown turns into 3-5x the request rate, payments-svc starts shedding load with 503s, and the retries feed themselves.

## Guardrails
- Any change to `checkout.retry_policy` needs a Payments reviewer and should roll out to a small percentage first.
- Watch payments-svc RPS against edge RPS after a change. If payments-svc RPS grows while user traffic is flat, revert the flag.

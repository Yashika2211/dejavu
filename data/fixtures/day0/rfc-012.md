# RFC-012: Consumer settings for notifications-worker

**Status:** accepted · **Author:** Marcus Oyelaran · **Reviewers:** Lena Fischer

## Settings
- Topic otp-requests, 12 partitions, consumer group notifications.
- `max.poll.records=500`, `max.poll.interval.ms=300000`.
- One consumer per partition at most; more than 12 pods only adds idle consumers.

## Why these numbers
A batch of 500 OTP messages must be processed within five minutes, so per-message processing has to stay well under 600 ms. OTP templates are rendered from in-memory templates; anything that does I/O per message will blow the poll interval and the consumer will be kicked out of the group.

## Operating notes
- Consumer lag climbing together with `kafka_rebalances` means members are leaving the group. Adding consumers makes it worse: every join triggers another rebalance.
- If processing got slower (a deploy), roll it back, or raise `max.poll.interval.ms` / lower `max.poll.records` while you fix it.
